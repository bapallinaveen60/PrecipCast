About PRECIP-AI

What is PRECIP-AI?

PRECIP-AI uses satellite observations and atmospheric information with artificial intelligence to classify precipitation conditions.

Data

PRECIP-AI combines satellite observations with atmospheric and terrain information to identify precipitation types across India.

  • INSAT-3DR — geostationary satellite imagery, the primary source for cloud observation
  • ERA5 — atmospheric reanalysis, providing background weather conditions
  • SRTM DEM — terrain elevation, used to account for the landscape's effect on rainfall
  • GPM-DPR — satellite rainfall radar, used for reference and validation

Understanding precipitation types

Convective Rain

Rainfall associated with strong upward atmospheric motion. It can produce intense rainfall and thunderstorms.

Stratiform Rain

Widespread, generally steady rainfall covering a larger area.

Shallow Rain

Light precipitation produced by relatively shallow clouds.

No Precipitation

No significant precipitation is expected at this location.
