Scientific Information

This page documents the data sources, model design and validation behind the forecasts shown on the main map. It is not needed to read a forecast — it exists for those who want to understand how one is produced.

Input data & channels

SourceRole
INSAT-3DR (VIS, TIR1, TIR2, WV)Cloud imagery and cloud-top brightness temperature
CAPEConvective Available Potential Energy — atmospheric instability, a driver of convective rain
TCWVTotal Column Water Vapour — moisture available for precipitation
SRTM DEMTerrain elevation — orographic influence on rainfall
GPM-DPRSatellite rainfall radar — reference labels for training and validation

Model

Classification is performed by a convolutional neural network with an attention mechanism over the INSAT channels, with CAPE and TCWV incorporated as physics-aware auxiliary features and DEM as a static terrain input. The model outputs one of four precipitation classes per grid cell along with a confidence score.
